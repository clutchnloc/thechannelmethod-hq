To earn ad revenue you need 1,000 subscribers plus one of these:

The 500-subscriber tier for fan funding and Shopping is not changing. That's a realistic first milestone, and Shopping is where your loc products come in.

30-day content bank

Nov 16 to Dec 15. Four long-form videos, each with 3 short clips, plus 14 stand-alone Shorts: 30 pieces in all. Built from the questions your clients ask, the myths you correct in your chair and the topics you can teach without research.

Titles are working titles. We'll tighten them, and your thumbnails, in your Content Bank review.

Filming system

Film while you work so content doesn't add time to appointments. Microloc and Sisterlock work lives in the details, so the overhead angle does most of the teaching.

Angle 1 · OverheadHands, grid and tool. The technique shot for parting, retie patterns and 3- vs. 4-point rotations.
Angle 2 · SideYou and your client together. Shows the care and the conversation, which is what you want to be known for.
Angle 3 · FrontYou talking to camera. Explain what you're doing and why. Start with 20-second takes.

Studio days

Mount the phone overhead before your first client. Grab 10–20 seconds per angle at each step: consultation, sectioning, the grid, the retie, the reveal.

House calls

A small mobile kit: phone, clamp mount or mini tripod, clip mic. Hands-only footage works well here and protects your senior clients' privacy.

Cross-posting routine

  • Publish the long-form video on YouTube first, with your booking link in the description.
  • Cut 3 clips of 30–60 seconds. Post them across the week to YouTube Shorts, Reels on _lovely_locs and TikTok on Locd.lovely1.
  • Every clip caption points back to the full video. Put your YouTube channel in your link in bio.
  • Say "Tampa" in your titles and descriptions so local search finds you.

Consent and privacy

Ask before you film, every time. Get a yes on camera or in writing, film hands and hair only for anyone who prefers it, and never show a client's face without permission. You just moved, so keep your house number, mail and location tags off camera, and give your address only after someone books.
